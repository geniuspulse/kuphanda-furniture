# Welcome to Kuphanda Furniture

At Kuphanda Furniture, we believe that great furniture starts with great craftsmanship. Located in Likuni, Lilongwe, we custom-make high quality office and home furniture using premium Malawian hardwood.

## What We Do

We specialize in:

- **Office Furniture** — Executive desks, boardroom tables, office chairs, and storage cabinets
- **Hardwood Doors** — Handcrafted double-face doors made from premium timber
- **Bedroom Furniture** — Bed frames and wardrobes in custom sizes and finishes
- **School & Church Furniture** — Durable desks and benches built for daily use

## Made to Order

Every piece is custom-made to your specifications. Choose your size, finish, and materials — we build it for you.
